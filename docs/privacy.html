Talewright Adventures: Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · Developer: Andro.Meta · Contact: Andro.Meta.Official@gmail.com

The short version: Talewright Adventures has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no servers. Your games stay on your device. To write the story, the game sends the story text to the AI service you choose, using your own key, straight from your device. We never receive it.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved games, heroes, settings and the AI keys you paste in.
  • The reading report (words a minute, scenes read or skipped) for each reader.
  • Any scene you report with the ⚑ button.

This is kept only in the app's storage on your device. It is not sent to us and is not included in Android cloud backup or device-to-device transfer. You can move your games yourself with Load Game → Export saves.

What is sent to your AI service

Each turn, the game sends the AI service you picked in AI Settings (Google AI Studio, Groq or OpenRouter) what it needs to write the next part of the story: the story so far, the choices made, your heroes' names and game stats, the world you chose or described, and the youngest reader's age (so the story's reading level and content fit). It is sent directly from your device over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), using the key you provided.

That service handles the text under its own privacy policy: Google, Groq, OpenRouter. Some free tiers may use what you send to improve their models, so please don't type real personal details (full names, addresses, phone numbers) into hero names or Divine Will.

Reporting a scene

The ⚑ button on the story lets you report a scene. The report is saved on your device and the storyteller is told to avoid anything like it for the rest of that game. Nothing is sent to us unless you tap "Email it to us", which opens your own email app with the report filled in.

Read-aloud

Reading the story aloud uses your device's own text-to-speech voices.

Children

Talewright Adventures is meant for players aged 13 and over. Younger children can play with a parent or guardian. The game adapts reading level and content to the youngest reader's age, and parents can see the reading report in Settings. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; we do not collect personal information at all.

Your choices

  • Delete a saved game from Load Game, or clear the reading history in Settings.
  • Remove an AI key from AI Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything it kept.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
