## Full description (4000 max)
Curl up for a story that's never the same twice.

Talewright Adventures is a cozy choose-your-own-adventure game with an AI storyteller. Pick a world, name your heroes, and every choice you make writes what happens next: the people you meet, the friends you make, the treasure you find, and the villain waiting at the end of the road.

✨ STORIES THAT GROW WITH YOU
• Pirates, space, haunted houses, dinosaurs, a wild west town, an underwater city and more, or describe your own world in a sentence
• A real adventure with a beginning, a middle and an ending: three acts that build to a final showdown, then a new chapter if you want more
• The story remembers your friends, your foes, and everything you've done

🎲 FIVE WAYS TO TRY ANYTHING
Be Brave, Clever, Sneaky or Kind, or try something gloriously silly and trust your Luck. Every choice shows your odds and how risky it is: Safe, Bold or Reckless. Bold moves pay off big… when they work.

⚔️ CLASSIC TURN-BASED BATTLES
Attack, defend, use items and unleash special moves that grow stronger the more you use them. Level up, raise your stats, find gear and lucky charms, and visit the shop between adventures.

👨‍👩‍👧 MADE FOR FAMILIES
• Up to four players take turns on one device
• The story adapts its reading level and content to the youngest reader in the group
• Read-aloud: have any scene read out loud
• A reading report for parents shows each reader's words per minute and whether they're reading the story or skipping ahead
• Report any scene with one tap and the storyteller will steer clear of it

🪄 DIVINE WILL
Want the story to go somewhere new? Write your own twist and watch the world change, even step through a portal into a whole new world.

🔑 FREE AI, YOUR KEY
The storyteller runs on free AI services. Paste a free key from Google AI Studio, Groq or OpenRouter once in AI Settings (the in-game guide shows you how) and the game picks whichever is working best. No accounts, no ads, no tracking: your games stay on your device.

Every choice writes the tale. Where will yours go?
