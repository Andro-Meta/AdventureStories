      How to Play

      Your turn

      Each turn you get five choices, one for each way of doing things. The icon shows the stat the action uses and the % is your chance:

      • 💪 Brave: fight, climb, face danger
      • 🧠 Clever: search, solve, figure things out
      • 🥷 Sneaky: hide, trick, slip past
      • 💛 Kind: help, talk, make friends
      • 🍀 Luck: something wild and silly. A long shot, but spectacular when it works!

      Danger

      No mark = Safe: if it fails, nothing hurts. ⚠ Bold: failing can hurt. ⚠⚠ Reckless: failing will hurt, but it pays the most.

      Rolls

      Every choice rolls a 20-sided die plus your stat. A natural 20 is a critical success; a 1 is a fumble. Even a setback gives XP.

      Stats and levels

      Stats go from 0 to 10. Use a stat successfully 6 times and it grows. Each level gives a point to spend: tap ⭐ on your hero card.

      Fights

      Attack hits a foe. Special uses a move or spell (spells cost MP). Item uses something from your bag. Defend halves damage until your next turn. Run tries to escape. Foes get tougher as you level, and so does the loot.

      Healing

      Potions and other items, Kind choices, rest spots on your journey, the inn in the Shop, and a breather after every won fight all bring back HP and MP.

      Items and lucky charms

      Equip weapons and armor from your Bag; use potions and other items. Lucky charms help 🍀 choices and make critical successes more likely. Two charms fuse: the best one keeps its luck and adds a fifth of the other's.

      The Shop

      New stock every 5 turns. Each hero can buy one of each piece of gear or charm; potions stay on sale. Sell from your Bag for half price.

      After you win

      Beat the villain to unlock god mode: write anything you want to happen, below the usual choices.

      Saves

      The game saves itself every turn. On the Load Game screen you can export your saves to a file and import them again.

      Sound & Voice

      Tap 🔊 on the story at any time to hear the current scene.

      About Adventure Stories

      Adventure Stories is an AI-powered, text-based RPG where your choices shape the narrative, guided by an AI storyteller.

      Create characters, explore themed worlds, battle monsters, find items, and complete your adventure goal! The storyteller is a free online AI (Google, Groq or OpenRouter keys, set once in AI Settings; the smart switcher picks whichever is working).

      Features: 13 themes plus your own, five approaches with Safe / Bold / Reckless danger each turn, stats and levels, fights that keep pace with you, rest spots and treasure, lucky charms that fuse, a shop, god mode after victory, and local saves you can export.
